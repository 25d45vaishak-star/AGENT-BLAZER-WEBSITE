ABOUT / THE WHY

A club for forward motion.

AgentBlazer is the Department of Computer Science and Engineering’s student-led platform for leadership, innovation and meaningful technical practice.

Our purpose

Make the frontier feel close.

The club turns emerging technology into approachable, shared experiences. Students meet the ideas, tools and people shaping the industry — and then get time to try them themselves.

LeadershipInnovationTechnical excellenceIndustry exposure

Club principle

Every session should leave a trace.

That trace might be a new skill, a useful connection, a sharper question or a prototype that did not exist before.
